# Customer Segment Recommendations

## VIP Customers

**Priority:** Platinum | **Customers:** 2,221

### Marketing Strategy
Exclusive invitation-only events, personalized wealth management offers, priority customer service.

### Retention Strategy
Assign dedicated relationship manager, quarterly portfolio reviews, premium loyalty rewards.

### Cross-Sell Opportunities
- Private banking services
- Investment portfolio management
- Insurance products

### Upsell Opportunities
- Platinum credit cards
- Premium mortgage products
- International banking

### Expected Impact
Retention of top 5% revenue contributors, potential 15-20% AUM growth.

---

## High Net Worth

**Priority:** Gold | **Customers:** 3,916

### Marketing Strategy
Targeted wealth advisory communications, tax optimization seminars, estate planning.

### Retention Strategy
Proactive outreach from senior advisors, competitive rate matching, fee waivers.

### Cross-Sell Opportunities
- Tax-advantaged investment accounts
- Estate planning
- Insurance bundles

### Upsell Opportunities
- Premium savings accounts
- High-yield CDs
- Commercial lending

### Expected Impact
Increase share-of-wallet by 10-15%, reduce attrition to under 3%.

---

## Dormant Customers

**Priority:** At-Risk | **Customers:** 3,863

### Marketing Strategy
Win-back campaigns with incentives, re-engagement emails, dormancy alerts.

### Retention Strategy
Account reactivation bonuses, personalized "we miss you" outreach, fee forgiveness.

### Cross-Sell Opportunities
- Simple savings products
- Mobile banking activation
- Automatic transfers

### Upsell Opportunities
- Active checking account incentives
- Cashback offers on reactivation

### Expected Impact
Reactivate 15-20% of dormant accounts, recover $2M+ in deposits.
