# Enterprise Customer Segmentation — Executive Summary

## Overview
The platform segmented **10,000 customers** into **3 distinct personas** using unsupervised machine learning.

## Key Findings
- **Highest Value Segment:** Dormant Customers (Avg Balance: $0.00)
- **Lowest Value Segment:** VIP Customers (Avg Balance: $0.00)
- **RFM Champions:** 499 customers
- **At-Risk/Dormant:** 2,504 customers

## Recommendations
1. Prioritize retention for VIP and At-Risk segments.
2. Launch reactivation campaigns for Dormant customers.
3. Invest in digital capabilities for Young Digital segment.
4. Cross-sell wealth products to High Net Worth customers.
