# Business Findings & Executive Summary

## Strategic Overview
The exploratory data analysis reveals crucial insights that demand immediate strategic action. The banking platform's portfolio exhibits concentrated areas of high profitability and specific segments with elevated risk.

## Core Business Findings

### 1. Revenue Concentration
- **Observation:** The North America and Asia Pacific regions account for the vast majority of our revenue.
- **Action:** Increase marketing budget allocation to these regions by 15% to capitalize on the momentum.

### 2. Profitability Drivers
- **Observation:** 'Premium Business Checking' and 'SME Credit Lines' are our most profitable products, contributing heavily to the bottom line despite a smaller user base.
- **Action:** Develop targeted upsell campaigns for existing corporate clients to adopt these premium products. Consider sunsetting low-margin legacy products.

### 3. Risk Exposure
- **Observation:** Default rates are concerningly high (7.2%) among new SME customers (under 1 year of operation) and low-income retail segments (8.5%).
- **Action:** Immediately revise the underwriting criteria for new SME applicants. Implement stricter credit scoring models incorporating alternative data points to mitigate this exposure.

## Summary Impact
By reallocating resources towards high-margin products and tightening risk controls on new SMEs, the enterprise can expect an estimated 8-12% improvement in net portfolio yield over the next fiscal year.
