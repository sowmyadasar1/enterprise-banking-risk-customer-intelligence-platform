# Business KPI Guide

This document defines the core Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) tracked by the Enterprise Banking Intelligence Platform.

## 1. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Definition**: The average cost spent to acquire a new customer.
- **Formula**: Total Marketing & Sales Expenses / Number of New Customers Acquired.
- **Data Sources**: Marketing budget tables, Customer dimension.

## 2. Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
- **Definition**: The total net profit a company can expect from a single customer throughout their relationship.
- **Formula**: Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) * Customer Lifespan - CAC.
- **Data Sources**: Transactions, Customer dimension, Product margins.

## 3. Churn Rate
- **Definition**: The percentage of customers who stop using the bank's services over a given period.
- **Formula**: (Customers lost during period / Total customers at start of period) * 100.
- **Data Sources**: Account status history.

## 4. Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- **Definition**: A metric used to gauge customer loyalty, satisfaction, and enthusiasm.
- **Formula**: % Promoters - % Detractors.
- **Data Sources**: Survey results.

## 5. Risk-Adjusted Return on Capital (RAROC)
- **Definition**: A risk-based profitability measurement framework.
- **Formula**: (Expected Return - Expected Loss) / Economic Capital.
- **Data Sources**: Loan portfolios, Risk models, Capital allocation.

## 6. Non-Performing Loan (NPL) Ratio
- **Definition**: The ratio of the amount of non-performing loans in a bank's portfolio to the total amount of outstanding loans.
- **Formula**: Total Non-Performing Loans / Total Outstanding Loans.
- **Data Sources**: Loan balances, Default statuses.
